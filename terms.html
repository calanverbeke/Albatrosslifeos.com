By default, the FA receives no Apple Health data. You can turn this off at any time. These numbers are for reflection only and are not medical information.

Reactions:

Reactions you apply to FA messages (double-tap to react) are stored in session memory on your device only. They are never transmitted to our servers and are cleared when the conversation session ends.
