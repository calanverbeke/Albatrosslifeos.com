The Short Version

Your data is yours. We do not sell it. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to profile you for third parties. The app is built to serve you — not to monetize you.

When you use the Flight Attendant AI feature, a limited, specific subset of your in-app data is sent to Anthropic's API to generate responses. This is disclosed fully below. If you do not use the Flight Attendant, no personal content leaves your device beyond what is needed to sync your account.

What We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. We do not store your password in plain text.

Profile data: Your first name and Flight Attendant name preference, which you provide during onboarding.

App content: Your journal entries, daily check-in responses, life wheel scores, habits, goals, and reflection answers are stored in your account database hosted on Supabase (see Third-Party Services below). This data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is protected by row-level security — meaning only your authenticated account can access your data.

Consent record: We record the date and time you agreed to this Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. This is required for legal compliance.

Voice recordings: If you record a voice memo or time capsule, the audio file is stored in your account so it is available on your other devices. Recordings are only uploaded when you save them, and are deleted when you delete them or delete your account.

Subscription status: Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm which plan you have; it receives your account ID and your purchase status.

Device identifier: We generate a random ID for each app install, used only to apply the one-device limit on free accounts. It is not your advertising identifier.

Basic in-app activity: We store a few simple things tied to your account — such as how many times you have opened the app and which introductory tips you have seen — so features like tips behave correctly. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Crash reports: If the app crashes, a crash report (the error, your device model, and OS version) is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it. Crash reports are not linked to your account.

What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

— Your journal entries or personal reflections for any purpose other than displaying them to you

Health Data

On iPhone and iPad, Albatross can optionally read three things from Apple Health — your sleep, your workouts, and your mindful minutes — to show them in your Daily Check-In. This is entirely optional: nothing is requested until you tap "Connect Apple Health", and you choose exactly what to allow.

By default this data is read-only and stays on your device. It is shown on your screen and nowhere else: it is not uploaded to our servers, not stored in your account, and not sent to anyone.

Optionally, you can turn on "Apple Health sharing" for your Flight Attendant (Menu → What Your Flight Attendant Knows). If you do, daily totals for the past 7 days — hours slept, workout minutes, and mindful minutes, never raw Health records — are included with your Flight Attendant requests and processed by Anthropic like the rest of the Flight Attendant context described below. A memory summary of a conversation may reflect them. Sharing is off by default, applies only from when you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

Apple Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is never sold. Albatross never writes anything to Apple Health.

You can change or turn off access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Albatross LifeOS. Health Connect support for Android is planned for a later release and will be added to this section first.

Calendar

If you allow calendar access, Albatross reads your device calendar so your existing events appear in the Planner, and adds events there when you schedule them.

Events you create in Albatross are saved to your account (title, time, and notes). For events that already exist on your device calendar, we save only the event's ID and the Life Wheel category you assign to it — not its title or details.

Calendar access is optional and can be turned off at any time in your device Settings.

Location Data

Albatross may request your approximate location solely to calculate local sunrise and sunset times for the circadian rhythm interface feature — the shift between morning navy mode, day white mode, and evening navy mode.

Your location is used only in the moment of calculation. It is not stored, logged, or transmitted. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Location access is entirely optional. You can use the app without it by using the manual day/night toggle.

The Flight Attendant and AI Processing

The Flight Attendant ("FA") feature uses the Anthropic Claude API to generate personalized responses. When you interact with the FA, a context payload is assembled from your in-app data and sent to Anthropic's servers to generate a response.

What is included in every FA context payload:

— Your life wheel category names and scores
— Your active habits and goals
— Your daily check-in data from the past 7 days, including morning identity statements, energy, mood, stress, habit completions, Top 3 task completions, evening scores, and small wins
— Your recent "Your Truth" responses from weekly and monthly reviews
— Your personality anchor paragraphs (generated during onboarding)
— Your ranked values
— Only if you turn on "Apple Health sharing": your daily sleep, workout, and mindful-minute totals for the past 7 days (totals only — never raw Health records)

FA Memory System:

Albatross stores AI-generated memory summaries of meaningful FA conversations in your account database on Supabase. After a substantive exchange with the FA, a secondary AI call is made to Anthropic to extract 1–3 sentences summarising what was learned about you. This summary is stored under your account and injected into future FA conversations so the FA can build genuine knowledge of you over time.

These memory summaries:

— Are stored in your Supabase account database, protected by row-level security
— Are accessible only to your authenticated account
— Are used solely to personalise your FA experience
— Are never used to train AI models
— Are never shared with third parties
— Can be deleted on request — email c.alan.verbeke@gmail.com and we'll remove them within 30 days, or they're removed automatically if you delete your account
— Are retained until you delete them or delete your account

Journal Consent Toggle:

By default, your journal entries — including your goal for the day, I Am declarations, gratitude entries, and your free-write journal body — are not included in the FA context payload.

You may choose to grant the FA access to your complete journal entries — including your free-write journal body — by enabling the journal consent toggle in Settings → Flight Attendant → Allow Journal Access. This toggle can be turned off at any time and takes effect immediately, and only applies going forward from when it's enabled.

Reactions:

When you react to an FA message (double-tap to react with ❤️ 😂 👍 🔥 💯), those reactions are stored in your device's session memory only. They are never transmitted to our servers, never stored in Supabase, and are cleared when you close the conversation.

Anthropics role:

All data sent to the FA is processed by Anthropic under their API terms of service and privacy policy. Anthropic does not use API-submitted data to train their models by default. You can review Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

If you do not wish your data to be processed by Anthropic, do not use the Flight Attendant feature.

Data Storage and Security

Your personal app content — journals, check-ins, scores, goals, habits, values, FA memory summaries, and Your Truth responses — is stored in your account database hosted on Supabase, protected by row-level security. This means only your authenticated account can read or write your data. No other user, including Albatross staff, can access your personal content through normal application channels.

Reaction data from the FA screen is stored in session memory on your device only. It is never transmitted or persisted.

Data read from Apple Health stays on your device unless you turn on Apple Health sharing with your Flight Attendant (see Health Data above).

All data transmission between the app and Supabase uses HTTPS encryption. Data is encrypted at rest on Supabase's infrastructure.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
